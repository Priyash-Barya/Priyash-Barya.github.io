--- title: "Integrated Nanophotonics" excerpt: |
  • Ultra-slow light: We use slow light in microring resonators to reduce the loss rates, which results in nearly a 3-order increase in the Q-factor of the resonator. See our Nature Communication paper
  • Photonic molecules: Using slow light resonator modes with electro-optic modulation, we plan to extend the lifetime of photonic molecules
  • Topological photonics with TFLN: collaborating with Prof. Hafezi's group on using multiple timescales to relax phase matching and enable poling-free nonlinear effects.
