--- title: "Integrated Nanophotonics" excerpt: "

Light travels fast and doesn't stay very long in nanophotonic devices due to propagation losses. We use extreme dispersion — engineered with coherent atoms doped into the material — to slow it down dramatically.

  • Ultra-slow light: a significant, record-high increase in resonator cavity lifetime, enabled by atom-induced dispersion.
  • Novel electro-optic effects: using electro-optic modulation to couple slow-light or polaritonic modes.
  • Topological photonics with TFLN: collaborating with Prof. Hafezi's group on using multiple timescales to relax phase matching and enable poling-free nonlinear effects.
" collection: portfolio link: /publication/2025-11-25-ultra-high-q-tunable-microring-resonators --- Light travels fast and doesn't stay very long in nanophotonic devices due to propagation losses. My work uses extreme dispersion, engineered with coherent atoms doped into the material, to slow it down dramatically. In resonators, one striking outcome of this is a record-high increase in cavity lifetime. I'm also exploring novel electro-optic effects, using electro-optic modulation to couple slow-light or polaritonic modes, and collaborating with Prof. Hafezi's group on topological photonics in thin-film lithium niobate (TFLN), using multiple timescales to relax phase matching and enable poling-free nonlinear effects.
