We fabricate silver nanoisland substrates via RF sputtering and characterize their plasmonic response through ellipsometric modeling, identifying Fano-resonant hybrid modes that arise from coupling between individual nanoislands. These substrates are evaluated for their utility in plasmon-driven photocatalysis and surface-enhanced Raman spectroscopy (SERS) sensing, with the modeling providing design guidance for optimizing nanoisland geometry for enhanced light-matter interaction.
