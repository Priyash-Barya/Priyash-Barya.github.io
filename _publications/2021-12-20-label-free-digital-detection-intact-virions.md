We introduce a label-free biosensing method for rapid, single-virion detection and quantification of intact virus in saliva. Aptamers capable of distinguishing active from inactive virions are immobilized on a photonic crystal surface, where captured virions are identified using interferometric scattering (photonic resonator interferometric scattering microscopy, PRISM). Applied to pseudotyped SARS-CoV-2, the approach tracks the motion of individual captured virions for highly selective recognition, achieving a limit of detection of 1x10^3 copies/mL at room temperature — a rapid alternative to conventional COVID-19 diagnostics.
