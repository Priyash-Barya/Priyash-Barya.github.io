By spectrally and spatially matching a plasmonic fluor nanoparticle to a photonic crystal resonator, we achieve a 52-fold enhancement in fluorophore emission intensity through combined near-field enhancement, improved extraction efficiency, and increased spontaneous emission rate. This boost in signal-to-noise ratio makes it possible to image and digitally count individual plasmonic-fluor tags, enabling detection of human interleukin-6 (IL-6) with a 10 fg/mL limit of detection and a seven-log dynamic range — roughly three orders of magnitude more sensitive than conventional ELISA.
