We present a low-cost, disposable pressure sensor built entirely on paper, using molybdenum disulfide (MoS2) grown directly on the paper substrate with graphite-pencil-drawn interdigitated electrodes. Integrated into a medicine container, the sensor detects each dosage event and relays the data to a companion smartphone app, allowing patients and their families to monitor adherence and discourage overdose or mishandling — all with a sensitivity comparable to devices made with far more sophisticated fabrication techniques.
