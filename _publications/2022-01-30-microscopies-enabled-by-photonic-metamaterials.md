This review surveys how photonic metamaterials — including plasmonic nanostructures, ring resonators, and photonic crystals — generate surface-localized, resonantly enhanced electromagnetic fields that boost contrast in biological microscopy. We cover enhancement mechanisms for excitation, emission extraction, absorption, and scattering, and discuss how these effects are being translated into practical microscopy techniques for label-free and fluorescence-based biosensing.
