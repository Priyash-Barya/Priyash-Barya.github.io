I am currently a PhD student at the University of Illinois Urbana Champaign (UIUC) working under Prof. Elizabeth Goldschmidt. I am experimentalist working in the area of quantum optics and interested in building technogies for future quantum information processing and networking. Previously, I received my MS degree from UIUC working with Prof. Brian Cunningham on using plasmonic-photonic coupling to enhance biosensing.
