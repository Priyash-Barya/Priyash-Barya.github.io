**2026**

- **August 2026** — *Telecom Quantum Memory over One Microsecond in Nanophotonic Lithium Niobate* accepted to **Nano Letters**, reporting a record efficiency–storage-time product for quantum memory on any integrated nanophotonic platform. [Preprint on arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.11588)
- **May 2026** — Presented "Integrated Nanophotonic Waveguides for Telecom-band Single Photon Storage" at **CLEO 2026**.
- **2026** — Named recipient of the **Paul D. Coleman Outstanding Research Award**, UIUC ECE Department, for the second consecutive year.
- **2026** — Awarded the **SPIE Optics and Photonics Scholarship**.
- **January 2026** — Delivered an invited talk on ultra-high-Q tunable microring resonators enabled by slow light at **SPIE Photonics West 2026**.
