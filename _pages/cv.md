Research experience
======
**Interests:** Quantum memory, quantum networks, materials for quantum, quantum nanophotonics

* **Aug 2022 - Present**: Joan and Lalit Bahl Fellow, Graduate Research Assistant, Physics Department, University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign
  * Advisor: Prof. Elizabeth A. Goldschmidt
  * Achieved ultra-high optical Q (~10<sup>8</sup>) tunable resonances in Er<sup>3+</sup>:LNOI microrings via slow-light-enhanced spectral-hole burning, a three-order-of-magnitude increase in Q
  * Experimentally discovered and theoretically modeled an intensity-dependent dephasing mechanism, driving Er<sup>3+</sup> toward the radiative limit (T<sub>2</sub> → 2T<sub>1</sub>) by decoupling it from the spin bath
  * Developed a telecom quantum memory with 1.1 µs storage, up to 2.9% efficiency, and 2.2 GHz bandwidth, with noise below a single photon — a record-high efficiency-storage time product on any integrated nanophotonic platform
  * Collaborating with Prof. Mohammad Hafezi on multi-timescale frequency combs in TFLN
* **Aug 2020 - Aug 2022**: Graduate Research Assistant, Electrical and Computer Engineering Department, University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign
  * Advisor: Prof. Brian T. Cunningham
  * Developed and characterized plasmonic-photonic hybrid experiments achieving 52× fluorescence enhancement and enabling single-protein detection of IL-6 down to ~10 fg/mL, three orders of magnitude lower than conventional ELISA
  * Performed electromagnetic simulations validating photonic-crystal-enhanced single-quantum-dot fluorescence for 10 aM biomarker detection of target DNA
* **Dec 2019 - Jul 2020**: Project Assistant, Centre for Nano Science and Engineering, Indian Institute of Science
  * Advisor: Prof. Ambarish Ghosh
  * Optimized and fabricated AZO plasmonic nanostructures via RF-sputtering and nanosphere lithography; identified Fano-resonant hybrid modes in silver-graphene dimers using COMSOL
